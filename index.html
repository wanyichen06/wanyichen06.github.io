Hi, I am Wanyi Chen (陈万毅), an M.Eng. student at the Natural Language Processing Center, Soochow University, expected to graduate in 2028. My research focuses on AI4AI and agentic post-training. I build verifiable systems that enable AI to participate in improving AI itself, while studying how data, training, environments, and evaluation can produce more capable long-horizon agents—toward increasingly autonomous and general intelligence.

I am currently working with XYZ AI Lab on AI4AI and Deep Research Agents. Previously, I was a research intern at Microsoft Research Asia and worked on LLM applications at ByteDance and NIO.
